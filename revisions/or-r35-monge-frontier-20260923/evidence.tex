\subsection{Same-input frontier acceleration}\label{sec:r35-evidence}
The Monge implementation targets the complete restricted-memory frontier on the \emph{same} renewal inputs as the unaccelerated global algorithm. Table~\ref{tab:r35-scale} reports actual oracle calls and wall times, not a comparison against an exponentially larger unfolding. Both methods use exact fractions and the same primitive moment representation. The new routine changes terminal and prefix search, including leftmost ties and the treatment of missing triangular entries. Every compared budget value agrees exactly.

The companion documents 98 exact instances, 1,712 frontier equalities, 1,712 controller replays, and an independent direct-enumeration comparison. Source-identified negative-input and boundary tests accompany the scale data. The unchanged piecewise-response and R34 global-frontier suites are rerun in temporary directories, leaving their earlier evidence intact. These tests support reproducibility and isolate the structural acceleration; they do not establish empirical service calibration, exhaustive literature priority, or superiority over an unimplemented specialized solver.
