\section{Evidence Semantics and Reproduction}\label{sec:evidence60}
The current study contains 90 distinct rational model specifications, 270 budget-tagged cases and 1,260 method requests. A method request is the unit of execution, not a statistically independent problem. Thirty seeds recur across the standard, exceptions and curved families, and each specification recurs at three time allowances. The exact source/input freeze, method order, all completed and interrupted records, original policies, certificates and environment receipts accompany the readers. The historical 288-request study remains a separate, immutable experiment with 56 distinct specifications and 72 tolerance-tagged cases.

\paragraph{Primary success criteria.} A rationally certified target requires an independently checked original feasible policy, an independently checked rational upper bound, interval width at most the declared additive tolerance, and completion within the total request allowance. A numerical-bound target is reported separately: SCIP supplies a numerical global upper bound for the exact algebraic formulation, and rational fixed-book reoptimization supplies an independently checked lower policy. Its reported numerical gap includes the stated floating-point consistency tolerance; it is not an exact global certificate. A deadline, missing certificate, checker failure or state limit is retained and is not counted as a rational success. A proof checked only after its request deadline does not retroactively satisfy that deadline.

\paragraph{Accounting.} The parent-process clock includes interpreter startup, model setup, optimization, serialization, compression and independent verification. Component clocks record the phases reached, so their denominators can differ. A missing phase is omitted from its conditional cost distribution rather than assigned zero. Uncompressed and compressed proof sizes are both measured. The exact post-study verification rechecks all complete certificates without changing the original timing records. The separate build receipt binds reader inputs and outputs; the final checkout receipt binds the published payload to its Git commit.

\paragraph{Cost drivers.} Deficit-grid size depends on the remaining capacity, the payoff slope bound, the command allowance and the requested tolerance. The exact verifier examines all predecessor transitions, so a fast optimized convolution does not imply a fast independent proof check. Price branching depends on whether a single active book supports the promise; different books with bracketing targets can leave a gap. Tariff compression enumerates exceptional-command subsets but has no resource-grid factor. These different costs explain why operation counts, proof sizes and deadline attainment are reported separately. The generator varies sizes, caps, fee exceptions, payoff curvature and tolerances jointly; it is not an orthogonal causal experiment, and no causal effect is inferred by regressing one factor on a dependent request count.

\paragraph{Structural verification.} Exact small-instance checks compare 172 tariff instances with 6,302 exhaustively enumerated books. Five additional large-rational cases include denominators of up to 513 bits. Forty-eight corruptions of the tariff proof are rejected by an independent checker. Graph tests cover 160 conservative graphs, 160 failed-conservation witnesses, 480 bounded-path comparisons with exhaustive paths, 400 exact repairs and 400 concave convolutions with holes. The positive price-gap example is checked with rational arithmetic. These finite checks complement the proofs; they are not substitutes for universal statements.

\paragraph{Flat submission package.} The current manuscript, companion and response have standalone sources with all scientific inputs expanded. The separate code/data archive exposes current code, a flat runtime, frozen source snapshots, row-level results and verification instructions without requiring a reader to infer a chain of revision-directory imports. Historical readers and their hashes are supplied separately. The source map distinguishes byte-identical execution snapshots from path-only adaptations in the flat runtime. Neither a reanalysis nor a packaging rebuild is relabeled as a new timing run.
