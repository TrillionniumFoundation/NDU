\section{Checker and Execution Instructions}\label{sec:r51-checker}
The selected-prefix checker imports only the Python standard library. It does not call the optimizer, an optimization solver, or the optimizer's support routine. It reconstructs each candidate union from the include/exclude decisions; verifies the anchor and mandatory levels; evaluates scalar supports at knots, bounds, and stationary points; and verifies the supplied forward and backward upper potentials. At every branching node it requires both include and exclude children. Every pruned or open leaf needs its own upper witness, and the final endpoint must dominate all surviving leaves. It independently evaluates the final policy in the uneliminated model, including every positive-probability support point and every opening charge. The experiment driver also verifies that each certificate's model equals its declared instance.

Compressed bytes mean deterministic gzip bytes for the saved certificate in that execution. Uncompressed bytes, counts of rational entries, maximum numerator and denominator bit lengths, checker times, and checker-to-search time ratios are all computed from the saved objects. Changing the problem requires revalidation; a winning book's smaller partition is not a substitute for coverage of competing books. Numerical mixed-integer solutions have a different evidence status and are never passed off as exact certificates.

From the repository root, the following commands reproduce the current experiment and readers. To measure fresh timings, use a separate clean checkout and keep the protocol unchanged; do not overwrite the committed record in the archival checkout.

\section{Capacity-Reservation Calculation}\label{sec:r51-capacity-data}
The demand-derived model in the main article is evaluated on three site states with probabilities $(1/4,1/2,1/4)$, caps $(3/10,11/20,4/5)$, engineering ceilings $(2/5,13/20,19/20)$, and backup curvatures $(0,2,7)$. The certified batch catalog is $\{0,1/5,2/5,3/5,4/5,1\}$, budget three, and the opening charge of index $i=0,\ldots,5$ is $i/100$. The contract promises nine tenths of the weighted cap. Uniform independent demand and unit revenue two imply $f(x)=2x-x^2$, so these data have $(r,q)=(2,2)$.

The exact optimum is $67/100$. The saved policy, selected book, supporting bounds, and original-space verification appear in \path{results/application.json}. For each used batch $x$, the check computes
\[
 2\int_0^1\min\{u,x\}\,du
 =2\left(\int_0^x u\,du+\int_x^1 x\,du\right)
 =2x-x^2
\]
using exact rational arithmetic, then averages over the implemented lotteries and subtracts backup and certification costs. This agrees with the canonical response, exhaustive fixed-book reference, and selected-prefix certificate. It is an independently formulated demand integral, not a fitted or observed application outcome.
