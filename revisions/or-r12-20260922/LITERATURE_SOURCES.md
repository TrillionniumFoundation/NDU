# Primary-source checks for the added R12 positioning

Checked September 22, 2026. Existing bibliography entries remain in the predecessor sources. These records identify new positioning sources and the distinction made in the manuscript; they are not a claim that the cited works already prove the new service-control results.

| Source | Primary record | Use in R12 |
|---|---|---|
| Altman (1999), *Constrained Markov Decision Processes* | Author's book manuscript: https://www-sop.inria.fr/members/Eitan.Altman/PAPERS/h.pdf | Resource states, occupation measures, and constrained-control foundations. |
| Spear and Srivastava (1987), *On Repeated Moral Hazard with Discounting* | https://doi.org/10.2307/2297484 | Promised-utility recursion; distinguished from public-state participation here. |
| Thomas and Worrall (1988), *Self-Enforcing Wage Contracts* | https://doi.org/10.2307/2297404 | Dynamic participation/self-enforcement precedent. |
| de Farias and Van Roy (2003), *The Linear Programming Approach to Approximate Dynamic Programming* | https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/opre.51.6.850.24925 | Approximation and performance-guarantee literature, not a claim of inventing residual analysis. |
| Tibshirani and Taylor (2011), *The Solution Path of the Generalized Lasso* | https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.1971 ; https://doi.org/10.1214/11-AOS878 | Matrix absolute-value penalties and duality are established; R12 specializes the balance to continuation prices/cuts. |
| Hoeffding (1963) | https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1963.10500830 | The bounded-validation concentration step is standard. |
| Satija, Amortila, and Pineau (2020) | https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/satija20a.html | Constrained-MDP value-based feasibility work. |

| Virtanen et al. (2020), SciPy 1.0 | https://scipy.org/citing-scipy/ ; https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-019-0686-2 | Numerical software attribution, not evidence of hardware-independent superiority. |
| SciPy SLSQP and HiGHS interfaces | https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/optimize.minimize-slsqp.html ; https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/optimize.linprog-highs.html | Named classical implementations and termination semantics. |
