## Exact claims of this revision

The four-component certificate is an explicit Fenchel-gap decomposition. Its model-specific diagnostic consequence is that fixed switching/participation slack cannot be removed by changing only resource and equality prices. Convex scalar conjugate minimization yields a small-dimensional price-repair problem; the rational acceptance safeguard guarantees monotonicity, not exact finite-step Newton convergence. The benchmark theorem is a max-gate comparison that links the adaptive expansion margin to implementation error. The statistical theorem applies Hoeffding and a union bound to lower observations of gain against the restricted optimum, with width twice the certified range. These statements are deliberately separated from claims of new general sensitivity, duality or concentration inequalities.

## Format source

Operations Research submission guidelines: https://pubsonline.informs.org/page/opre/submission-guidelines . The current package uses anonymous letter-size 11-point text, one-and-a-half spacing, one-inch margins, a one-paragraph abstract of at most 200 words without mathematical notation, an introduction without mathematical notation, author-year alphabetized references, and tables after references. Actual page counts are checked for the Lengthy category, and the companion is no longer than the main PDF. This is preparation for review, not approval of authorship or journal acceptance.
