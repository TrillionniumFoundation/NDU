# R42 primary-source and scope audit

All sources were checked on September 24, 2026. Older manuscript references are retained rather than replaced by this list.

1. Operations Research submission guidelines, https://pubsonline.informs.org/page/opre/submission-guidelines . The manuscript uses the lengthy category, not an assumed unlimited article format: generally no more than 40 pages excluding references; companion no longer than the article; 11-point type, 1.5 spacing, one-inch margins; abstract at most 200 words; equation-free introduction; author-year references and tables after references. The manuscript uses an article class implementing these specifications, not a false claim to use a particular journal class.

2. Jourdain B, Pagès G (2021), Optimal dual quantizers of 1D log-concave distributions: Uniqueness and Lloyd like algorithm, Journal of Approximation Theory 267:105581. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jat.2021.105581 ; primary preprint https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.10816 . Optimized one-dimensional dual grids and the quadratic Lloyd-like algorithm are antecedents. The new paper does not claim those generic ideas; its eligible supports, endogenous contractual targets, and heterogeneous one-sided costs define the distinction.

3. Aardal K, van den Berg PL, Gijswijt D, Li S (2014), Approximation algorithms for hard capacitated k-facility location problems. Primary preprint https://arxiv.org/abs/1311.4759 , version 4. Opening a facility has a fixed charge separate from assignment/service charges; this is an antecedent for interpreting selected-level certification costs. No new generic fixed-charge selection model is claimed.

4. Del Pia A, Dey SS, Molinaro M (2017), Mixed-integer quadratic programming is in NP, Mathematical Programming 162:225–240. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10107-016-1036-0 ; author manuscript https://www2.isye.gatech.edu/~sdey30/QIP_NP.pdf . Rational quadratic witnesses and linear-system arguments are classical. The stationary-face lemma is proved self-contained, including singular maximizing faces; novelty is not attributed to generic rational quadratic optimization.

5. Stouffer K and coauthors (2023), Guide to Operational Technology (OT) Security, NIST SP 800-82 Rev. 3. https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.800-82r3 . Sections 5.2.5.1 and 6.2.4.1 and control CM-7 support component-level allowlisting, least-functionality and restricted-interface analogies. Relevant PDF pages were visually inspected. They do not document deployment or economic calibration of the manuscript's whole renewal agreement. Revision 3 is cited as a dated source, not described as the latest standard; the NIST site also listed a 2026 revision-4 draft.

The computational claims are supported by the revision's executed rational results, not by external literature or counts of successful checks as a proxy for importance. No literature source is asserted to subsume the entire contractual model, and no negative claim of “no prior work” is made.
