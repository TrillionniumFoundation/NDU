The unique optimal tier vector must match the supplied policy. It then reconstructs the recursive child planes, checks every scalar box conjugate at the policy, verifies the zero shared-table coefficient and exact root upper value, and checks every anchor plane against every declared release. The example's domain-wide certificate is established by the proof, not by these 81 point checks.

A second exact master calculation maximizes the minimum of $u-1/8$ and $7/8-u$ over $0\leq u\leq1$. These are valid one-period shared-table planes with root promise $1/2$, reward and curvature one, and zero release. The maximum $3/8$ occurs at $u=1/2$. This check keeps the table master explicit rather than assuming every plane has zero table coefficient. The adaptive-cell test reconstructs all correlated feasibility and objective coefficients from the declared map in EC Section~\ref{ec:adaptiveuniform}. The linear release-cost decision is checked at its stationary release $1/15$ and against the exact piecewise-concave frontier.

The fourteen negative controls include: childwise table substitution, a false pooled optimal value, an incorrect release-radius factor, a violated participation witness, incorrect conditional-price normalization, a corrupt recursive release coefficient, endpoint-only adaptive feasibility, a hidden midpoint violation, a stale active-face proposal that violates another cap, a singular active basis, negative cached inequality prices, a broken root promise, an infeasible continuation witness, and an unsupported tolerance declaration. \subsection{Precisely specified stress family}
Each independent block has root payment $x_0+(x_L+x_H)/2=1$, tiers in $[0,1]$, conditional child constraints $a_Lx_L\leq b_L$ and $a_Hx_H\leq b_H$, and objective
\[
 p_0x_0-x_0^2/2+(r_Lx_L+r_Hx_H)/2-(x_L^2+x_H^2)/4.
\]
The restricted class shares one child tier, $x_L=x_H$. Blocks are independent renewal/service components; a configuration with $C$ cycles and $S$ services has $n=3CS$ tier variables. Each block has its own exact payment commitment. This separability makes an exact fresh comparator available and exposes overhead fairly; it is not a dense or long-horizon complexity experiment. There is no new neural model, empirical calibration, or fitted test distribution.

For parameter dimension $d$ and query index $i=0,\ldots,11$, set
\[
 \theta_j=\frac{((i+3)(7j+5)+3j^2)\bmod19-9}{9},\qquad j=0,\ldots,d-1.
\]
For block index $b=0,\ldots,CS-1$, put
\[
 z=\frac1d\sum_j\frac{((b+1)(j+2))\bmod7-3}{3}\theta_j,
 \quad
 z'=\frac1d\sum_j\frac{((b+4)(j+5))\bmod9-4}{4}\theta_j.
\]
Primitives are $p_0=1+z/8$, $r_L=1/5+z'/4$, $r_H=9/5+z/2$, $a_L=1+z/8$, and $a_H=1-z/10$. In the stable-cap design, $b_L=7/20+z'/30$ and $b_H=7/10-z'/30$. In the unstable-cap design, $b_L=1/2+\theta_0/5+z'/20$ and $b_H=1/2-\theta_0/5-z'/20$. Coefficients and bounds therefore change together, and actual query coefficients enter every certificate. Effective child caps lie strictly between zero and one. The root remains feasible throughout the declared design.

Let $v_f=(1-p_0+(r_L+r_H)/2)/2$. The exact restricted tier is
\[
 v^*=\min\{\max(0,v_f),b_L/a_L,b_H/a_H\},\qquad
 x^Y=(1-v^*,v^*,v^*).
\]
All measured cases have $0<v^*<1$. With $\nu=p_0-(1-v^*)$ and total effective cap price $2(v_f-v^*)$, assign that nonnegative price to a binding smallest cap, dividing by its actual coefficient $a_j$; set the other cap price to zero. The pooling price is determined by low-child stationarity. This is an exact primal--dual pair, including at the declared tie convention. It is used as a fresh reference but never interpreted as a unique economic directional derivative at degeneracy. Full policies are generated by exact weighted water filling under the same payment and cap constraints and independently checked through their KKT conditions.

\subsection{Governance, timing, and memory}
An initial cache of size $k_a$ uses the same parameter formula at indices $100,\ldots,100+k_a-1$. The best aggregate upper bound is selected over these global block-price vectors. A restricted witness repairs that anchor's child tier by clipping it to the actual two child caps and adjusting the root to maintain payment one. This is not reoptimization. If the upper-minus-witness width exceeds $CS/1000$, the controller refreshes with a new audited exact restricted pair and evicts the oldest anchor; otherwise it retains the cache. The cache remains at its declared size. A query's exact restricted solve is evaluation-only unless the declared trigger refreshes; on a refresh its full measured solve/audit time is charged. The fresh baseline always computes that same exact pair. Both pipelines meet the same requested tolerance, and the exact fresh comparator in fact has zero error.

The independent replay regenerates query primitives, checks all cached dual bounds and their nonnegative coordinate/participation decompositions, verifies every implemented policy and restricted witness, reconstructs each FIFO update, and confirms each trigger and resulting error.
