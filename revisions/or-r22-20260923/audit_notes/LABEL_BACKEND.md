The new scaling labels use the lifted formulation of the same full objective at numerical tolerance 1e-9. Every label must independently certify a gap at most 1e-5. A dense-label engineering pilot was stopped after it revealed avoidable offline computation; its incomplete timing observations are not used in the paper. Context seeds, uniform 64-label budget, fitted architectures, targets and held-out contexts were unchanged, and no predictor was selected. The archived R16 frozen models used in the principal comparison are untouched.
The initial engineering plan used 128 labels. The final reported scaling design uses 64 per configuration, uniformly, to bound offline work; all ten configurations and every declared held-out context remain. This amendment is disclosed rather than called an execution of the original 128-label plan.
