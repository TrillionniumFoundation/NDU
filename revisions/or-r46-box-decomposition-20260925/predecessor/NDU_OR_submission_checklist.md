# Operations Research — R45 submission checklist

The paper is prepared as a lengthy anonymous manuscript using 11-point type, one-and-a-half spacing and one-inch margins. The text-only abstract is under 200 words. The introduction uses no mathematical notation. Subject classifications, three keywords and the Optimization review area appear on the title page. Author-year references are alphabetical. Tables follow the reference list; the analytic figure is near its appendix discussion.

Final page counts, source identities and zero-reference/overfull checks are in `BUILD_VALIDATION.json`; they are not inferred from an old PDF. The companion must not exceed the current main paper's total length. Nonreference main pages must not exceed forty.

The latest R44 report is answered point by point in the revision response. New theory and exact/numerical evidence are distinguished. The charged core-method counterexample remains visible. Neither an unrestricted-branch FPTAS, a factor-two necessity theorem nor field calibration is claimed.

All 34 earlier mathematical label identifiers are retained in the current main/companion. The original scientific directories remain unchanged. Old root readers are snapshotted. The content map identifies archived historical study material, and the preservation manifest checks the pinned review base.

The author must still complete journal submission declarations (authorship, conflicts, related submissions, funding and appropriate editorial metadata) in ScholarOne. This revision does not submit to the journal or attest personal declarations on the author's behalf.
