# R24 literature and contribution boundary

The manuscript retains the R23 bibliography and result-level comparison with its cited primary literature. R24 does not add a citation merely to label a standard algebraic fact as new. The benchmark-relative transfer result is proved directly and presented as an accepted-service structural consequence, not as a newly invented generic flow or duality principle.

| Established tool | Manuscript attribution | R24 distinction |
|---|---|---|
| Convex optimality, normal cones, conjugates and curvature bounds | Boyd and Vandenberghe (2004), together with the paper's explicit proofs | Continuation-specific prices, actual participation-slack coordinates, and implemented economic accounting |
| Graph total variation and its dual/path representation | Tibshirani and Taylor (2011); Arnold and Tibshirani (2016) | Subtree caps, customer participation, exact restricted-comparator coordinates, and constructive accepted deviations |
| Constrained stochastic control and promised utility | Altman (1999); Spear and Srivastava (1987); Thomas and Worrall (1988) | Public-state service participation, not private-information screening or a new dynamic-programming principle |
| Prescriptive prediction, downstream decision loss, optimization layers | Elmachtoub and Grigas (2022); Bertsimas and Kallus (2020); Amos and Kolter (2017) | An explicit accepted expansion target and full implementation/comparator error charges |
| Learned dual information and repeated parametric optimization | Kraul et al. (2023); Stellato et al. (2020); Sambharya et al. (2024) | Matched comparisons against structure-aware continuation; no generic warm-start invention or guaranteed neural speed claim |
| Robust counterparts and uncertainty/performance tradeoffs | Ben-Tal and Nemirovski (2000); Bertsimas and Sim (2004) | A common comparator outer set is required when acceptance itself changes; its slack is now measured directly |
| Independent bounded-variable concentration | Hoeffding (1963) | The economic target is a fixed implemented rule against an optimized restriction; exact certificates supply conservative observations |

The operations-research contribution is the accepted-control structure and the connection to implemented gains. Numerical replay licenses recorded feasibility and bounds, not novelty. The paper makes no claim of an exhaustive priority review for every possible flow reformulation.
