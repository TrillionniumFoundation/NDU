`check_package.py` compares all **72** predecessor theorem/proposition/lemma/corollary/assumption/proof blocks against the current main-plus-companion sources. Its only normalization is the documented robust theorem-to-lemma environment change. The original result-level novelty table is revised to reflect the new contribution; every original empirical `tabular` block is checked without numerical rewriting.

The formal journal submission consists of the main paper and **one** electronic companion. The computational PDF is a readable record inside the separate code/data archive (which also contains raw observations and executable scripts), not a proposed additional formal companion. No essential proof has been moved to that archive to evade the companion length limit. The full historical record remains accessible to the next referee.
