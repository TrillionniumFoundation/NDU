A renewal provider chooses a limited menu of terminal commands and allocates an accepted obligation across heterogeneous histories. We give an exact selected-boundary decomposition for finite catalogs with opening charges, expected caps, realization ceilings, and convex intermediate-service costs. Each adjacent selected pair owns terminal-reward increments and the service costs of histories whose eligibility ends there. An eligibility-independent capacity potential permits exact restoration of the original obligation after rounding. For rational quadratic primitives, the resulting additive approximation scheme is polynomial in inverse normalized accuracy, with no eligibility-dependent exponent and no enlargement of the command budget. Exact common-prefix optimization remains a stronger special case. A uniform command-deletion envelope permits simultaneous safe screening and transfers independently checked global intervals from reduced catalogs to their original instances. We also establish threshold-stability results and a compatible-production extension. Synthetic studies cover strict interior prefixes through 1,024 histories, controlled eligibility splits, difficult historical models, numerical mixed-integer comparisons, and certified catalog refinements. The screening study preserves every exhaustive optimum while exposing the cost of verifying exclusion witnesses. The contribution is the structural reduction and original-obligation guarantee, not universal solver-speed superiority.
