\subsection{Certified screening under catalog refinement}\label{sec:study53}
The additional experiment isolates Corollary~\ref{cor:transfer53}. Its protocol was committed before the screening runs, after the theoretical derivation and inspection of the earlier results. It is a controlled synthetic validation, not a statistical claim about operational frequency. Twelve cases cross 4, 16, and 64 histories with zero, two, four, and eight inserted commands. The base catalog is $\{0,1/4,1/2,1\}$; inserted commands are $1/2+i/20$ with charge $1/2$. Histories have cap $1/4$, uniform weights, curvatures cycling through zero to three, and distinct ceilings strictly between $1/2$ and one. The promise is $1/8$, the command budget is two, and the requested absolute width is $1/10$. The fine-accuracy and larger-history studies above remain separate and unchanged.

Table~\ref{tab:screen53} reports all twelve cases. Every inserted command satisfies the anchor-envelope test, whereas none satisfies the incumbent-based gross exclusion test $\rho(z)>f(1)-v_0$ at the exact incumbent. The rule also deletes the zero-charge endpoint one, which is ineligible for every history. Thus all reduced catalogs have three commands; this additional deletion is reported, not suppressed to retain the planned base-catalog size. Exhaustive optima before and after screening are all $15/64$. The full-catalog eligibility count reaches nine, while the reduced catalog retains one class. Bellman storage falls from 49--177 states to 33, without changing the root promise or budget.

The transfer certificate includes the original larger instance and its exclusion witnesses. It can therefore be larger than the unscreened certificate on the smallest cases; screening is not a universal compression claim. Both optimization and checking costs are displayed. The independently implemented checker deliberately enumerates neighboring contexts, so its cost need not decrease with the resource-table size. All measured times describe the recorded environment and are not extrapolated to large industrial instances.

Separate rational regressions exercise 120 seeded models, 2,332 arc-capacity identities, 542 full-context envelope maxima, and 129,448 individual target comparisons. Exhaustive optima agree before and after screening in every model; 149 commands are removed. Twenty reduced-catalog global certificates transfer successfully to their original instances. Fourteen altered certificates fail, including false exclusion bounds, attempted anchor removal, missing deletions, incorrect model binding, incomplete Bellman coverage, and invalid original lottery probabilities. Zero caps, zero promises, zero curvatures, exact threshold equality, and equality-charge deletion are included. These finite checks support implementation consistency; the universal claims rest on the proofs.
