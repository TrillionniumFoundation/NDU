In particular, it does not infer a tight bound for $C_*=\sum_v c_v$ beyond the reached-state bound already implied by the fixed-query price set.

All R31 theorem, proof, literature, computational, and negative-evidence files remain unchanged on this branch. R32 is an additive reviewability layer.
