The fee projection uses one-dimensional least trimmed absolute deviations: retain a fixed number of observations and minimize their absolute distance from a common location \citep{Tableman1994,Zioutas2015}. Consecutive retained windows and their medians are established preprocessing, not a new general clustering algorithm. Objective-coefficient perturbation is likewise a sensitivity principle; broader optimal-value sensitivity frameworks include \citet{XuBurer2017}. Our contribution is their integration with the tariff frontier: choose a sparse-exception surrogate, solve it exactly, and transfer its policy to the original feasible allocation with a certified additive value interval. The fees here are specified coefficients, not samples from an unknown distribution. Neither statistical estimation nor distributional robustness is asserted by this transfer.
