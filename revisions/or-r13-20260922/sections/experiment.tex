\section{Nonsmooth service decisions and frozen learning pipelines}\label{sec:r13-experiment}
The experiments separate structural computation, candidate quality, and complete deployment. They do not use the generality of a theorem to imply empirical coverage of every admissible model. The earlier noncentered, boundary-inclusive and nonlinear studies remain intact in the companion; this section exercises nonzero absolute switching and the new verified-cell construction.

\subsection{A declared service-control distribution and operational units}
Consider binary scenario trees with discount factor $0.9$ and equal branch probabilities. Thus $w_n=(0.9/2)^{\operatorname{depth}(n)}$. Tiers lie in $[0,1]$, the installed tier is $1/2$, and external net-payment coefficients are $c_n=w_n[0.8+0.1(n\bmod5)]$, with nodes numbered from zero. The accepted polytope includes every nonroot continuation cap and the root payment equality. Physical service and stock are held at their identified benchmark levels; the experiment concerns the remaining tier decision, not an untested inventory-learning claim.

The incremental profit, in profit units, is
\begin{align}\label{eq:r13-data-objective}
 G_{\alpha,\beta,\lambda}(x)={}&\sum_n(g_{0n}+\alpha g_{1n}+\beta g_{2n})(x_n-\tfrac12)
             -\sum_n w_n(x_n-\tfrac12)^2\notag\\
 &-\tfrac1{10}\sum_{n\ne o}w_n(x_n-x_{\operatorname{pa}(n)})^2
             -\lambda\sum_n w_n|x_n-x_{\operatorname{pa}(n)}|,
\end{align}
where the root's predecessor is $1/2$ in the last term. Three fixed vectors describe net marginal resource offsets after the tariff; each has zero sum. Their exact rational coordinates and generating seed are recorded. Therefore the \emph{reoptimized} fixed-tier comparator is exactly $1/2$ for every context: a common tier change has zero linear gain and strictly positive quadratic cost. This balanced-shock class is different from, and does not replace, the earlier noncentered class with boundary comparators.

One profit unit is the coefficient of a squared full-tier deviation in the per-review maintenance cost. The smooth inter-review cost has coefficient $0.1$, and $\lambda$ prices an absolute unit of reconfiguration. Adding a common level cost can make all gross resource costs positive without changing decisions. Results are divided by the discounted review mass $S_0=\sum_n w_n$. Multiplying all monetary primitives by a currency conversion multiplies every profit and certificate by that conversion; no such conversion is a field calibration. For example, a gain of $0.003$ represents $0.30$ per discounted review if one profit unit is defined as $100$ currency units. No operational population is asserted to have that calibration.

\subsection{Learning, verification, and actual refinement}
The learning experiment uses 31-node problems. Context coordinates are independent uniforms on the rational grid $\{-1,-0.999,\ldots,1\}$; friction is independently uniform on $\{0.01,0.011,\ldots,0.20\}$. We solve 1,024 training contexts, recover candidate bases, and retain \RThirteenCells\ distinct verified cells. A 192-unit random-feature ReLU layer, with a fitted multiclass ridge readout, proposes cell indices. Its scalar experts and value gradients are given by Theorem~\ref{thm:r13-cells}. The classifier learns joint continuation and switching patterns; it is not the earlier derivative-weighted regressor, and no superiority of derivative supervision is inferred.

We freeze three static-gated policies: check the first neural proposal, check the first three neural proposals, or check the cell of the nearest training anchor. Each returns the first region-valid proposal after exact flow repair and certificate calculation, comparing its reward with static. If no candidate survives, it returns static. Validation does not enlarge the library, update features, or tune a stopping rule. The complete model hash is recorded before and after both validation cohorts.

A separate 48-context matched-accuracy test adds actual classical refinement whenever the selected policy has normalized gap above $10^{-7}$. It includes a cold epigraph quadratic program and sequential checking of the same frozen cell library. Table~\ref{tab:r13-matched} includes generation, membership, exact audit, and all refinement time. The first-proposal face accuracy and added iteration counts distinguish learned candidate quality from a classical rescue. The data also contain raw face masks, switching signs, distances, and the acceptance and switching leakage of 96 neural/nearest proposals. Finite differences check the scalar experts' context-gradient pullbacks; these numerical checks supplement, not replace, the envelope proof.

Offline construction and fitting cost \RThirteenOffline\ seconds in the recorded environment. Under the observed mean timing difference, neural lookup recovers this cost after \RThirteenBreakEven\ repeated queries relative to cold optimization; this is an accounting calculation, not a forecast or an advantage over sequential cell checking. The small, fixed-dimensional context distribution makes such reuse possible. The cell library can grow rapidly with problem complexity. Timings are single-environment descriptive measurements, and sequential checking is the stronger latency comparator in this run. The positive learning result is verified reuse with explicit rejection and acceptance guarantees, not a claim that the neural router dominates classical lookup.

We then fix a model-derived range using the four deterministic forcing corners at the minimum friction, without altering any policy. The confirmation plan is saved before a second, independent 4,096-context cohort is drawn. This prospective second cohort is conditional on the already frozen model and the declared range calculation; it is not a refit on the first cohort.

\subsection{Retained evidence and independent audit}
The noncentered nonlinear study remains scientifically material: 48 instances, 432 deployments, boundary comparators, severe raw actor failures, and slower learned pipelines than cold SLSQP at matched accuracy. Its full text and tables are in Section~\ref{sec:r12-experiment}; no unfavorable row is removed or pooled with the new distribution. The first four frozen predecessor seeds were an economical transfer diagnostic, not a representative sample of training variability. The inconclusive derivative-weighting comparison remains inconclusive. The earlier 992-coordinate failures and their actually executed fallbacks also remain available.

The new standard-library-only verifier independently reconstructs \RThirteenPolicies\ rational policy certificates, \RThirteenChecks\ continuation checks, all corner ranges, and 12 rational critical-friction brackets. It imports neither the optimizer nor a numerical linear-algebra library. Optimizer flags, approximate face masks, and hardware timings are not treated as certificates. All data are synthetic; the mathematical scope, fixed-context validation scope, and previous transfer evidence are kept distinct.
