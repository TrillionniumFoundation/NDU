\section{Implementation details for verified continuation cells}\label{ec:r13-implementation}
All essential new proofs are in the main paper. This section specifies the algorithms sufficiently to reproduce their numerical implementation. The ordinary Python sources contain no service calls and use pinned NumPy and SciPy versions. Training, validation, confirmation, structural tests, and matched deployment use separate recorded seeds. The confirmation stage does not change the frozen trained model.

\subsection{Sparse Lipschitz bounds without fill-in}
For the scaled operator $M$ in \eqref{eq:r13-feasibility}, write $k_i=\kappa_i$, let $d_i$ be the number of children of node $i$, and let $b_i=(k_i+\sum_{j:\operatorname{pa}(j)=i}k_j)/c_i$. The absolute row sum for row $n\ne o$ is $b_{\operatorname{pa}(n)}+b_n$. The root column sum is $d_ok_o/c_o$. For a nonroot column $j$, with parent $p$, the absolute column sum is
\[
 k_j\left\{\frac{d_j+1}{c_j}+\frac{d_p+\mathbf1_{\{p\ne o\}}}{c_p}\right\}.
\]
To check these formulas, expand $M_{nj}$: its contributions are positive at the parent of $n$ and the children of $n$, negative at $n$ and its siblings. The two contributions at $n$ have the same sign and add. Counting their occurrences yields the displayed sums. Thus both induced norms, and a valid Lipschitz upper bound, are obtained in linear time even on a star. Multiplying separate norm bounds for the two factors instead is valid but can lose the tariff/weight cancellation and make the step unnecessarily small.

Each trial starts from zero tensions. Accelerated projected gradient uses the standard momentum recursion and clips to the trial tension box. Every twentieth iteration, the implementation checks the separating direction and the two-pass repair. It maintains the best bracket, not the latest optimizer residual. The final lower direction and repaired upper tension are serialized as rational numbers and checked by a separate program. At the 12,000-iteration limit the bracket is still valid, but a width above $10^{-4}$ is a failure to meet the requested numerical accuracy. No asymptotic runtime estimate is fitted to these twelve instances.

\subsection{Cell recovery and rejection}
Training starts with an explicit epigraph quadratic program. A candidate active set is only a proposal. Pivoted factorization removes redundant equality rows, and a dual feasibility linear program recovers nonnegative inequality prices and capacity-feasible switching tensions. Positive inequality prices and unsaturated zero-edge tensions supply a candidate independent basis. Saturated zero-edge tensions may instead be represented by a signed cell. Every resulting affine map is checked against all primal and dual region inequalities. Failure to find a verified basis is recorded rather than assigned a fabricated label.

The input coordinates are scaled by $(1,1,10)$. The random hidden weights are standard normal; biases are uniform on $[-1,1]$. Features concatenate a constant, the three scaled inputs, and 192 ReLU outputs. The multiclass readout is fitted by ridge least squares with coefficient $0.01$ against one-hot cell labels. All random features and coefficients are serialized. The neural policy checks its ordered first one or three cells. The nearest-anchor comparator uses Euclidean distance in the same scaled context coordinates and then checks that anchor's cell. Sequential lookup tests the same cells in their fixed training order. It receives no different library or optimal query-time face.

Numerical membership permits a $2\times10^{-9}$ inequality tolerance solely as a proposal filter. The deployed flow is rounded to denominator $10^9$, negative flow coordinates are set to zero, and a common radial scaling enforces both tier bounds. Exact subtree feasibility survives this scaling. Nonnegative prices and capacity-feasible tensions are then rounded/clipped, and Theorem~\ref{thm:r13-leakage} is evaluated in rational arithmetic. Thus the numerical region tolerance is not the final feasibility or optimality tolerance. Wrong switching signs contribute leakage, including when rounding opens a nominally fused edge.

\subsection{What each timing and face statistic measures}
The matched test measures complete pipelines, including exact audit and any classical rescue. Rescue uses a cold feasible start; a rejected region is not credited with unexecuted warm-start savings. The data separate its refinement iterations from initial cell verification. Distance is measured from the first affine proposal to the separately certified reference policy. Binding-constraint masks and switching signs use a $10^{-6}$ diagnostic tolerance and are not proofs that a mathematically active face has been identified. The independent verifier instead checks their associated repaired policies and all prices. The first sequentially tested cell is fixed, so its first-face accuracy has a different interpretation from the learned first proposal.

Offline amortization divides library-construction plus fitting time by the observed mean cold-minus-neural online time, when positive. It excludes no observed online audit cost, but it does not include hypothetical future library maintenance. It also does not turn one timing sample into a confidence statement. The complete matched records allow recalculation under a different deployment volume or hardware cost.

\subsection{Independent sampling and range reconstruction}
Training uses seed 13031 and random features seed 13032. The first validation uses 13033 and matched deployment 13034. After the deterministic corner range is saved, confirmation uses 13035. Within each cohort the two forcing coordinates and friction are independently generated from the specified finite uniform grids. Samples are not resampled when routing fails: the prescribed static policy is retained, and its certificate contributes to the mean. The first broad-range cohort remains in full. The second range is derived from the model's four forcing vertices, not from truncating unfavorable sample values. For each vertex the standard-library verifier checks both its policy and its upper bound before accepting the range.

The final confidence statement is conditional on the frozen model, distribution, and monetary normalization. Its positive gain floor compares with the static outside arrangement. The simultaneous regret ceilings are intentionally reported alongside it: they remain much wider than high-accuracy deployment tolerances. This distinction prevents the validation theorem from being described as a blanket certification of neural training, distribution shift, or superiority over classical control.
