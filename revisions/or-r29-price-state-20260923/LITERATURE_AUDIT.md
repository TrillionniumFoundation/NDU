## Operations Research submission format

Official guidance: https://pubsonline.informs.org/page/opre/submission-guidelines . The manuscript uses anonymous presentation, 11-point type, 1.5 spacing, one-inch margins, an abstract of at most 200 words without citations or formulas, author–year references, and tables after references. Mathematical notation is not introduced in the Introduction. No footnotes are used.

The regular category is at most 30 manuscript pages excluding references. This preservation-oriented package is designated **Lengthy Manuscript**, for which the published guidance normally allows up to 40 pages excluding references. The electronic companion must not exceed the manuscript length. The checker reports total pages and a conservative nonreference count rather than silently treating the package as a regular manuscript.

This is an author/referee repository revision, not an actual submission through the journal's submission system. Author disclosures, conflicts, cover letter, and editorial category acceptance are not fabricated or submitted.
