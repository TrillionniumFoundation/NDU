# Operations Research reader checklist — R34

Reference checked September 23, 2026: https://pubsonline.informs.org/page/opre/submission-guidelines

## Reader format

- Intended category: Lengthy Manuscript. The journal generally allows 40 pages excluding references; this build conservatively allows at most 40 pages including all material. No submission has been made.
- 11-point letterpaper source, one-inch margins, 1.5 line spacing; no type-size reduction to accommodate new theorems.
- Anonymous title page, subject classification, area of review, and three keywords.
- One-paragraph text-only abstract: 190 words in the prepared source, checked automatically against a 200-word maximum.
- Introduction without equations or mathematical notation; new results explained in operational terms before theorems.
- Full new structural, global-algorithm, and institutional proofs in the main article; implementation details in the electronic companion.
- Author–year citations and alphabetized reference list retained. Tables collected after references, without vertical rules.
- References, citations, duplicate labels, and overfull boxes checked in the final compilation logs.
- Electronic companion no longer than the main article. Exact final page counts and PDF hashes are in the R34 BUILD_VALIDATION.json.

## Scientific and data audit

- Latest independent report and exact reviewed/base commits are identified.
- R34 results are distinguished from inherited R31–R33 results.
- Exact rational global-frontier, exhaustive-comparator, grid, and controller-replay tests are included.
- R33 exact response/circuit/KKT checks are rerun without overwriting inherited evidence.
- All numerical examples are declared synthetic. No specialized published solver run or empirical calibration is fabricated.
- The source-preservation manifest and predecessor wrappers retain earlier content.
- Root wrappers, proofs, tests, response, source identity, and newly generated reader PDFs must all be present before calling this a published revision.

## Author-side items not certified by this build

Author identities and affiliations, ORCID, conflicts of interest, funding, prior submission details, recommended editors/referees, exclusive submission, and ScholarOne declarations require the authors' factual confirmation. No financial-conflict statement, journal submission, or editorial approval is invented here.
