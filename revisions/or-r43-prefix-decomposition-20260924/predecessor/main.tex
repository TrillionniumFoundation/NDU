{\Large\bfseries Limited-Memory Renewal Contracts:\newline Participation, Quantization, and Exact Design\par}
\vspace{.3in}Anonymous manuscript for Operations Research\par\vspace{.2in}
\begin{minipage}{\textwidth}\textbf{Abstract.} A renewal provider must allocate accepted obligations through a limited execution alphabet. We solve the resulting joint design problem with endogenous branch promises, continuously chosen terminal levels, bounded realization exposure, and charges for the actual enabled levels. A branch-specific eligibility prefix gives the exact response to any promise. For quadratic operating costs and nonnegative quadratic level charges, eliminating lottery probabilities yields closed quadratic cells and a finite exact global algorithm. A second theorem rounds codewords and repairs their probabilities while preserving every branch promise and risk ceiling exactly. Its uniform error bound gives certified continuous-value intervals and an additive approximation scheme for a fixed alphabet budget. We retain the sharper saturated structure: cap-anchored nonhighest levels, a continuously optimized off-cap highest level, and contractual tail costs that remain Monge after minimization, permitting classical matrix search. Exact-alphabet thresholds, institutional-gap stability, homogeneous repeated-cap aggregation, and a charged interior pathwise-randomization example clarify the decision implications. Synthetic parameter studies compare continuous and catalog optima and separately measure the new algorithms. Established quantization, facility-opening, and quadratic-optimization principles are distinguished from the contractual reductions.
\par\vspace{.15in}\textbf{Keywords:} renewal contracts; quantization; dynamic programming.
\par\vspace{.1in}\textbf{Subject classifications:} Dynamic programming: service design; Programming: resource allocation.
\par\vspace{.1in}\textbf{Area of review:} Optimization.
\par\vspace{.1in}\textbf{Submission category:} Lengthy manuscript.
\end{minipage}\vfill September 24, 2026\end{titlepage}
