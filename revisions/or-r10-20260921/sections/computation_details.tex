\section{Nonlinear study: reproducibility and audit details}\label{ec:r10-replay}
The executable entry point is \texttt{revisions/or-r10-20260921/reproduce.sh}. The verification-only entry point is \texttt{verify.py}; it reads serialized rational inputs and does not import the optimizer or the neural feature code. Training, deployment, raw timing, structural-test, and static-gate records are separate files. Every deployment includes its full rational tier vector, nonnegative multiplier vector, exact value, exact static comparator value, and exact upper-gap fraction. Every training endpoint includes an analogous record. The former R7 accepted hierarchy, customer-exit, and quadratic portfolio records are separately regenerated and replayed; they are not relabeled as nonlinear results.

The binary tree has transition probability one half and discount factor nine tenths. Maintenance curvature is three halves, temporal adjustment is three fifths, and each service tariff cycles through one, five quarters, and three halves. Training uses quartic coefficient one and graph strength one quarter. Cycle and random-edge tests use those same coefficients; geometric tests use coefficients two and one half; weighted-block tests use three and one. The last graph has within-block connection probability seven tenths, cross-block probability twelve hundredths, and integer edge weights from one to four. Connectivity is not imposed or required for strong convexity. Primitive resource shocks are bounded rational numbers and centered with the actual discounted occupation weights.

The learned potential applies softplus nonlinearities at three scales and seven shifts to each of three local transforms: identity, one smoothing operation, and two smoothing operations. Its 63 readout coefficients are learned by regularized least squares. Both objectives receive the same 72 scalar oracle values per training seed. The derivative-weighted objective adds 24 central-difference equations computed from those same values. The fixed ridge coefficient is one hundred-thousandth. Ten training seeds and two problem-generation seeds per family are disclosed in code. No fastest seed, favorable instance, or failed transfer is selected for reporting.

Nonlinear Newton steps use the exact quartic Hessian. The participation dual is optimized with nonnegative multipliers; the dual's floating-point exit status is retained only as a diagnostic. Newton--PCG precomputes one shifted baseline Hessian factorization per graph--tree instance and reuses it across changing Newton systems and all 15 repeated solves. Thus the direct method's changing factorization cost is compared with an actual cached alternative, rather than treating repeated factorization as the only classical baseline. Setup is recorded separately; feature construction, acceptance repair, multiplier proposal, and fraction-based audit are included in the online candidate timings. Training time is separately retained and is not amortized into a claimed break-even point.

The exact repair first clips tiers to the compact box and rounds downward to a grid with denominator one hundred million. Because tariff entries are positive, multiplying all nonnegative tiers by the smallest subtree-budget ratio preserves the box and makes every payment inequality feasible. A second downward rounding preserves feasibility. The repair is deliberately simple, not claimed to be the closest feasible projection; its economic distortion is visible in the raw rewards. Consequently the static gate compares the repaired proposal with the exactly optimized static protocol before deployment.

The replay checker reconstructs the entire polynomial gradient from the recorded primitives, including both appearances of every temporal edge and both endpoints of every spatial edge. It reconstructs each ancestor multiplier contribution independently. No numerical tolerance is used for acceptance, multiplier signs, objective equality, or equality of the stored rational certificate. Structural checks additionally compare 120 edge-criterion cases with normalized linear programs, verify the tree summation identity with fractions, check 12 absolute-friction cases, and replay the zero-friction promise example with gap one thirty-second. These finite checks exercise the theorem implementation; the proofs do not rest on those tests.

A preliminary unanchored-readout run prompted the explicit reporting of severe-shift failures and the exact static gate. The final fitting architecture, sampling seeds, and regularization were not tuned to remove those failures. The final dataset differs from the preliminary calculation in replacing unchecked floating oracle values with feasible rationally certified values and explicit endpoint error bounds. Both value-only and derivative-weighted objectives receive this same change. All final negative cases remain disclosed; statistical inference concerns ten paired training-seed means, not a fictitious sample of 160 independent fitted networks.
