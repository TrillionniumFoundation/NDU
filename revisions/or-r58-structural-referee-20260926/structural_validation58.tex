\section{Additional Structural Verification}\label{sec:structuralvalidation58}
The additional R58 regression is separate from the timed comparative study. Its fixed design uses 24 seeds, four histories, seven catalog commands, two prototype groups and all seven command budgets. The original and prototype problems share every feasibility input. Rewards have either zero or positive dispersion around the prototypes; all service coefficients in the saturated-frontier tests are positive. Exact book enumeration supplies original and prototype optima. Every recovered original policy is independently checked.

For approximate types, the regression checks the weighted oscillation certificate, the four-command bound, exact prototype optimality, the resulting original regret, and invariance to history-specific additive reward constants. For saturation, a single frontier computation per model is checked against exact enumeration at each of seven budgets. All 168 budget certificates are verified by the independent deficit checker. Twenty-four off-saturation requests and 24 altered upper bounds are rejected. The complete rational inputs, policies, bounds and certificates are retained in \path{results/STRUCTURAL_R58.json}; these records test implementation, not a statistical performance claim. The universal statements and their assumptions are proved in Theorem~\ref{thm:robusttypes58} and Corollary~\ref{cor:saturation58}.
