### Full and partial regeneration

The anonymous submission bundle has no DOI assigned; `manifest.json` records SHA256 hashes and row counts, and `check_ndu_reproducibility_results.json` records the latest checker output. The full regeneration script is included for transparency. The full PyTorch-AD HBO/NBO experiment rerun can be regenerated with `python reproducibility/scripts/run_source_nbo_full_experiments.py --seeds 30 --train-steps 450 --width 56 --depth 2 --batch 96 --actor-lr 5e-6 --critic-lr 0.0012`, followed by `python reproducibility/scripts/make_nbo_solver_audit.py`, `python reproducibility/scripts/make_hbo_residual_diagnostics.py`, and `python reproducibility/scripts/make_hbo_residual_weight_audit.py`. ### What to expect

- **Anonymous artifact**: upload the full source zip as the blind reproducibility artifact; the PDF intentionally contains no public DOI or author-identifying URL.
- **Primary gate**: `python reproducibility/check_ndu_reproducibility.py` validates hashes, row counts, seeds, packaged CSVs, and manuscript headline values without retraining.
- **Light regeneration**: audit-builder scripts regenerate derived CSVs, exact-DP rows, SKU-panel outputs, theta policy surfaces, direct-P/Z diagnostics, direct-P/learned-Z solver-representation diagnostics, neural direct-P/learned-Z retraining diagnostics, residual diagnostics, the targeted residual-closing audit, the sampled greedy-action-gap audit or its deterministic no-torch archived replay, the validation-cover replay certificate, the neural empirical-envelope audit, the neural interval-domain certificate audit, the neural interval-sensitivity/refinement audit, the bounded-architecture Lipschitz budget and stress-margin audits, the neural guard cross-artifact consistency audit, refinement/stress audit, holdout/bootstrap audit, concordance audit, leave-one-out audit, bounded-composition envelope audit, stress-frontier audit, package self-replay certificate, local environment/dependency audit, lightweight replay dependency-closure audit, the analytic uniform HJB/greedy certificate audit, the validation-cover stability guard audit, the generalized-RL non-collapse audit, the state-augmentation/reward-shaping closure audit, the policy-class face hierarchy audit, the referee full-support synthesis audit, the strong-form claim-closure audit, the selected equal-budget Pareto confirmation, and the inventory normalized-residual Pareto audit from packaged inputs.
- **Full regeneration**: CEM and HBO/NBO reruns use the seeds, budgets, and hyperparameters listed below; these are optional and substantially slower.
- **Data provenance**: `data/sku_monthly_demand_source.csv` and `data/sku_monthly_demand_source_license.txt` record the public SKU-panel source/license; the AirPassengers trace is packaged for offline replay.

Dependencies are listed in `requirements.txt`. The lightweight checker is intended for routine artifact validation; full regeneration is optional and can be much slower than the checker. On the reference Linux/x86-64 environment used to create the bundle, the 30-seed same-budget CEM rerun took about 12.5 minutes, the small neural conditioning validation took about 5 seconds, and the HBO/NBO rerun is the expensive actor--critic diagnostic because it trains 12 method/benchmark policies across 30 seed blocks before held-out rollout evaluation. Full regeneration may produce tiny floating-point differences across numerical environments. For journal submission, this directory is designed to be uploaded anonymously as the reproducibility artifact; no public DOI or external URL is embedded in the blind manuscript.
